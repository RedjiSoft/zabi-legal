# Privacy Policy for Zabi

**Last updated: September 29, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Zabi ("Зъби") is an Android application designed to help users maintain a regular tooth-brushing routine through reminders and personal brushing history.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application accesses and how that information is used.

## 2. Health Data

Zabi may request permission to read sleep data provided through Huawei Health Service Kit.

The application requests **read-only access to sleep data** for the sole purpose of determining when the user's sleep period has ended. This information is used to schedule the morning tooth-brushing reminder after a delay configured by the user.

Zabi does not request permission to write, modify, or delete sleep or other health data.

The application does not use Huawei Health data for advertising, profiling, marketing, or medical diagnosis.

## 3. Other Health Data

Zabi does not request access to heart rate, blood pressure, location, body composition, stress, blood glucose, SpO2, reproductive health, or other health information that is not necessary for the sleep-based reminder feature.

## 4. Data Storage

Tooth-brushing status, reminder settings, and dentist appointment information used by the application are stored locally on the user's device.

Sleep data obtained through Huawei Health Service Kit is used only as necessary to determine the timing of the morning reminder.

Zabi does not sell users' personal or health information.

## 5. Permissions

Health data access is optional and requires authorization from the user.

The user may deny or revoke access to Huawei Health data. The application can continue to provide its other reminder functions without sleep-data access.

Zabi may also request Android permissions necessary for its reminder functionality, such as permission to display notifications.

## 6. Medical Disclaimer

Zabi is a personal reminder application and is not a medical device.

The application does not provide medical or dental diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

## 8. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the Zabi application, users may contact the developer through the GitHub repository where this Privacy Policy is published.
